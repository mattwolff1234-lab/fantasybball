Pickups

Grab a free agent or bring a player back from IR, even after the day's first game, as long as his own game hasn't tipped off. It goes straight onto your ESPN team.

How it works

  1. Find your player. Anyone whose game hasn't tipped off yet can be added, even after the day's first game.
  2. Pick who you're dropping. Anyone whose game has started is locked until tomorrow.
  3. Pick who sits tonight. ESPN won't let you put a late pickup in your lineup yourself, so the page does it for you.
  4. Confirm. He's on your ESPN team straight away.

Bringing someone back from IR works the same way: tap “Back from IR”. ESPN's rules still apply: 4 pickups a week, and players on waivers need a claim in the ESPN app.
