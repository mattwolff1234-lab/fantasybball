How this is ranked

Each player's projected stats for this season are run through our league's scoring. Players are then ordered by how many points they add over a free-agent pickup across the games they are expected to play, so a star who misses time drops a little.

The numbers start from ESPN's projections, blended with a second projection source for the top 200 players, and are then corrected. Grading ESPN's last three preseason projections against what really happened showed two habits: it expects about 10 more games per player than they end up playing, and it runs a few points per game too high. So every player's games are trimmed, and his points per game are blended with what he actually scored last season.

The stat line in each player's pop-up is ESPN's own projection. ESPN does not project double-doubles or split rebounds into offensive and defensive, so those are estimated from last season. "Steal" means ESPN's own ranking has the player at least 8 spots lower than this guide does; "reach" means at least 8 spots higher.
