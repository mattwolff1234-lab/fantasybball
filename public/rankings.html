How it works

Expected

Points a game from here on. It blends how he's playing this season with his preseason projection, and the projection counts as 4 games. We tested that on last season: after a dozen games, how a player has actually played tells you much more about the rest of his season than any preseason guess.

Value

Expected points a game above a free pickup, times the games his NBA team has left. A free pickup is the 5th best free agent on that same measure.

What it leaves out

Injuries. A player who is out is ranked as if he plays every game left, so check the red tag. Preseason numbers come from our draft guide; anyone who wasn't in it uses ESPN's projection.
